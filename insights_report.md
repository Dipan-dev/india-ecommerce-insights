# Business Insights Report
## E-Commerce Sales Analysis — 2023

**Prepared by**: Dipan Shil  
**Period**: January 2023 – December 2023  
**Dataset**: 3,000 orders | 500 customers

## Executive Summary

The e-commerce business generated **₹44.02 Lakhs** in revenue from **2,338 delivered orders** across 10 Indian cities in 2023. Electronics dominates revenue share, UPI is the leading payment method, and customer retention is identified as the primary growth opportunity.

---

## 1. Revenue Performance

- **Total Revenue**: ₹44,02,152 from delivered orders
- **Average Order Value**: ₹1,883
- **Peak Month**: Revenue is fairly distributed across the year with a slight uptick in festive months (Oct–Nov)
- **Recommendation**: Plan targeted promotions around Q3–Q4 to capitalize on the festive demand surge

---

## 2. Category Analysis

Electronics is the top-grossing category, followed by Home & Kitchen and Clothing. Books and Beauty have lower ticket sizes but high order volumes.

**Recommendation**: Bundle low-value categories (Books + Electronics accessories) to increase average basket size.

---

## 3. Order Quality — Returns & Cancellations

| Status      | Count | Share  |
|-------------|-------|--------|
| Delivered   | 2,338 | 77.9%  |
| Cancelled   |   270 |  9.0%  |
| Returned    |   252 |  8.4%  |
| Pending     |   140 |  4.7%  |

- **Return rate of 8.4%** is within industry norms (8–10% for Indian e-commerce)
- **Cancellation rate of 9%** is slightly elevated — commonly driven by COD orders
- **Recommendation**: Introduce a pre-cancellation confirmation flow for COD orders to reduce cancellations

---

## 4. City-Wise Performance

Top 3 cities contribute a disproportionately high share of revenue, while Tier 2 cities (Jaipur, Lucknow) show strong growth potential.

**Recommendation**: Targeted regional campaigns for Tier 2 cities with city-specific offers could unlock untapped demand.

---

## 5. Payment Behaviour

UPI is the most adopted payment method, consistent with India's digital payments trend. Cash on Delivery still holds a significant share, especially in Tier 2 cities.

**Recommendation**: Incentivise prepaid orders (UPI/Credit Card) with small cashbacks to reduce COD logistics cost and cancellation risk.

---

## 6. Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)

| Segment   | Description              | Action                           |
|-----------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Champion  | 10+ orders               | Reward with exclusive perks      |
| Loyal     | 5–9 orders               | Upsell premium categories        |
| Potential | 2–4 orders               | Re-engage with targeted offers   |
| One-Time  | 1 order only             | Win-back campaign via email/SMS  |

- The majority of customers are **one-time buyers** — this is the biggest retention gap
- **Recommendation**: Launch a loyalty program with points-based rewards to convert one-time buyers into repeat customers

---

## 7. Discount Strategy

Medium discounts (11–20%) generate the highest average order value, while heavy discounting does not proportionally increase revenue.

**Recommendation**: Cap discounts at 20% and focus on value-adds (free shipping, gift wrapping) rather than deep price cuts to protect margins.

---

## 8. Strategic Priorities (Top 3)

1. **Retention > Acquisition**: Most customers buy only once. A simple loyalty/reward program could significantly boost LTV.
2. **Reduce Cancellations**: A 9% cancellation rate costs real revenue — a small UX intervention could recover ₹2–3L annually.
3. **Invest in Tier 2 Cities**: Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai are already strong — the next growth frontier is Jaipur, Lucknow, Ahmedabad.
